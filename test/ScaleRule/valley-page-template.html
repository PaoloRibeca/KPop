Cutting the tree

Every sequence joined below the cut height shares a cluster. Agreement with the CDC labels as adjusted Rand index (ARI), homogeneity (each cluster within one class) and completeness (each class within one cluster). The best cut is the highest ARI over 120 heights.

Against KPopPhylo’s trees

KPopPhylo’s sparse neighbour-joining tree, built on the same axes, set beside the average-linkage (UPGMA) tree above. For each group of sequences — the clusters a valley cut produces, and the CDC classes — the share of members in groups that form an exact clade of the tree, and how closely the best-matching clade overlaps the group (Jaccard). Robinson–Foulds distance compares the two trees’ branches over the labelled sequences.

Pairs. 100,000 random pairs of the CDC-labelled spectra (seed 17), the same draw the structure test makes. Distances are Euclidean in principal coordinates over the first d axes of a correspondence analysis.

Samples. The analysis behind each projection used 244 VP1, 200 RdRp or 220 VP2 spectra. Uniform: chosen at random. Stratified: the autotuner's rule applied to the CDC classes, one spectrum per class and the rest by size, centre first then extremes. Cores: the same allocation, taking each class's members nearest its centre.

Histograms. 200 bins up to the 99th percentile of the pair distances; on the log scale, from the 0.1th percentile of the positive ones. The bars and the curve are drawn from 100,000 sampled pairs, and the smoothing control chooses how much the curve is smoothed — a drawing choice, nothing more. The valleys are not found here. They come from the calibrated detector, which works on all pairs of the same spectra: it smooths by one bin either side, simplifies peaks and troughs in order of relative size — a trough against the lower of its flanking peaks, an interior peak against the modes beyond the troughs it sits between, so a bump inside a gap is measured against the modes the gap separates rather than splitting it — and keeps what has a prominence of at least 3 resampling standard deviations over 50 resamples of the sequences, with between 0.2% and 90% of pairs below it. Because the drawn curve is a sample and the detector reads every pair, a valley can sit a little off the visible minimum. The test line is the structure test’s own rule instead: one valley, accepted when the dip reaches half the height of the smaller mode.

Trees. Average linkage (UPGMA) over every labelled spectrum in the same d axes, by a nearest-neighbour chain with Lance-Williams updates. The tree does not depend on the detector, so only where it is cut changes. A cut at a height takes the clusters standing there and scores them against the labels by adjusted Rand index, homogeneity and completeness; best is the highest-scoring cut anywhere on the tree, over a grid of 120 heights, which no valley is asked to know about. KPopPhylo: sparse neighbour-joining (--snj-mode periodic-rebuild) on the same projection, keeping the first d axes through the metric threshold, on one thread.

Labels. VP1's finest label is the drift variant within GII.4 and the genotype elsewhere. VP2 histograms were read from a 4,216-spectrum database, restricted to its 3,021 labelled spectra, all of which belong to the 3,027-sequence corpus of the earlier runs; its KPopPhylo tree was built on that corpus alone.
